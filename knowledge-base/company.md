## Team Structure

Wall-V operates with a hybrid model combining AI agents and human experts:

- **AI Agents** — Handle first-line work including code generation, testing, documentation, research, and repetitive tasks. This dramatically reduces timelines and costs.
- **Human Experts** — Handle complex architecture decisions, creative design, client communication, and quality assurance. Human oversight ensures every deliverable meets professional standards.

This model allows Wall-V to scale output without proportionally scaling headcount, passing the efficiency gains directly to clients.
