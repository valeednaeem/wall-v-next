# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How much does a website cost?

**A:** Our plans start at $499 (Starter), $1,499 (Professional), and $2,999+ (Enterprise). Custom quotes are available for projects with unique requirements. Contact our sales team for a tailored estimate.

### Q: Can I get a custom plan?

**A:** Yes, contact sales@wall-v.com for custom packages. We build tailored solutions for businesses that need specific features, integrations, or support levels beyond our standard plans.

### Q: Is there a money-back guarantee?

**A:** Yes, we offer a 14-day full refund on all plans. If you are not satisfied with our services within the first 14 days, you can request a complete refund.

**A:** Projects are split into milestones with individual payments. A typical split is Discovery 15%, Design 20%, Development 35%, Testing 15%, and Launch 15%. Each milestone has clear deliverables and client approval before proceeding.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?

**A:** We accept Stripe (credit and debit cards), PayPal, 2Checkout, bank transfers, JazzCash, and EasyPaisa. NET-30 payment terms are available for Enterprise clients.

### Q: Do you support multiple currencies?

**A:** Yes, we support USD, EUR, GBP, and PKR. Currency selection is available during checkout.

### Q: How long does development take?

**A:** Timelines vary by plan: Starter takes 2-4 weeks, Professional takes 4-8 weeks, and Enterprise takes 8-16 weeks. The actual timeline depends on project scope, feature complexity, and client feedback turnaround.

**A:** Our primary stack includes Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, MongoDB, and Tailwind CSS. For AI features, we use OpenAI and Anthropic models. We select the best tools for each project's specific requirements.

### Q: Can AI build my website?

**A:** Our AI assistants help discover requirements, generate demos, and accelerate development. Human experts oversee quality, architecture decisions, and final delivery. AI enhances our process but does not replace skilled developers.

### Q: Do you develop mobile apps?

**A:** Yes, we develop cross-platform apps using React Native and Flutter starting at $4,999. Native iOS and Android development starts at $19,999. We recommend cross-platform for most use cases due to cost efficiency.

### Q: How do milestones work?

**A:** Each milestone has defined deliverables, a timeline, and a payment amount. You approve each milestone before we proceed to the next. This ensures transparency and control throughout the project.
