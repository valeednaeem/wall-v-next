# Client Dashboard

The client dashboard is a comprehensive management interface with 26+ sections covering projects, CRM, support, billing, and administration.

## Dashboard Modules

### Dashboard Home

Overview page displaying key statistics and metrics. Provides a high-level summary of active projects, recent invoices, open support tickets, and account activity.

### Projects

Full project lifecycle management with stages:

- **Planning** - Project scoping and requirements
- **In-Progress** - Active development
- **Review** - Client review and feedback
- **Testing** - Quality assurance
- **Completed** - Delivered and closed

Each project tracks milestones, deliverables, timelines, team assignments, and budget.

### Tasks

Task management with:

- Subtask breakdowns
- Time tracking per task
- Assignee management
- Priority and status labels
- Due date tracking

### Invoices

Invoice generation and management:

- Line item breakdowns
- Tax calculations
- Payment status tracking
- PDF generation
- Recurring invoice support

### Orders

Order management with full lifecycle tracking from placement through fulfillment.

### Hosting

Hosting account management for client-hosted services. Includes resource usage monitoring and renewal tracking.

### Domains

Domain registration and DNS management. Supports domain lookup, registration, transfer, and DNS record configuration.

### Support

Support ticket system with threading. Clients submit and track issues through resolution.

### Messages

Internal messaging system for direct communication between team members and clients.

### AI Assistant

Built-in AI helper for content generation, suggestions, and automated tasks.

### Notifications

In-app notification system for alerts, updates, and activity feeds.

### Downloads

Digital product download management with access history.

### Subscriptions

Recurring billing and subscription management.

### Licenses

Software licensing management with activation and renewal tracking.

### Billing

Billing overview aggregating invoices, payments, and subscription status.

### Teams

Team management with role assignments and permission controls.

### Settings

- **General** - Account and site configuration
- **Profile** - User profile and avatar management
- **Security** - Password, 2FA, session management
- **Payment** - Payment gateway configuration
- **Pricing** - Pricing rules and discount management
- **Legal** - Terms, privacy policy, and legal document management

## CRM Module

### Leads

Sales pipeline management with stages:

1. **New** - Initial lead capture
2. **Contacted** - First outreach completed
3. **Qualified** - Lead meets criteria
4. **Proposal** - Proposal sent
5. **Negotiation** - Terms under discussion
6. **Won** - Deal closed successfully
7. **Lost** - Deal lost

Features lead scoring based on engagement, fit, and behavior.

### Clients

Client record management supporting three types:

- **Individual** - Single person accounts
- **Business** - Company accounts
- **Enterprise** - Large organization accounts

Each client record includes project history, total spending, communication log, and account status.

### Inquiries

Multi-type inquiry management:

- **Contact** - General contact form submissions
- **Support** - Support-related requests
- **Sales** - Sales inquiries
- **Partnership** - Business partnership requests

Each inquiry has priority levels and routing rules.

## Support System

### Ticket Structure

- Unique ticket numbers for tracking
- Priority levels: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Urgent**
- Category-based routing to appropriate teams

### Status Workflow

```
open -> in-progress -> waiting -> resolved -> closed
```

### Features

- Message threads with file attachments
- Internal notes (not visible to clients)
- First response time tracking
- Resolution time metrics
- Satisfaction rating on close
